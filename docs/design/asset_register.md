# Asset register

## Interactive blueprint and component structures — 2026-10-10

The blueprint SVG paths in `BlueprintDrawing.tsx` are newly authored code-native drawings inspired by the supplied multi-view layout, not copied stock artwork. The ivory/green drafting grid is CSS. `aircraftCutaway.ts` derives disposable translucent shell and outline geometry from the existing illustrative fighter GLB, preserving its asset provenance and cached resources. Interior component assemblies and wing ribs are code-authored conceptual geometry. `componentStructure.ts` authors conceptual section assemblies with installed Three.js primitives for twenty synthetic catalogue types (fifteen assembly families). No external CAD, texture or engineering model is downloaded. Locations in `componentArchitecture.ts`, dimensions and internal arrangements use arbitrary scene units and are not validated installation or physical-fault data. Unknown type codes have no fabricated structure. The red highlight represents recorded whole-component attention only.

## 1:1 light-fighter model and cutaway — 2026-10-11

`tejas-visual.glb` was rebuilt (revision `visual-v2`) in metres at 1:1 scale. Overall length, span and height match the publicly stated Tejas Mk1 figures (13.2 m, 8.2 m, 4.4 m; wing area ~38.4 m²), and a unit test asserts them. Fuselage superellipse sections, the ~62°/50° compound-delta planform with thin NACA-section wings, separate elevons, slat line, wingtip rails, lateral intakes with splitter plates, single-piece canopy, ejection seat and displays, fin and rudder, drag-chute housing, convergent-divergent nozzle with afterburner liner, three-per-side pylons, a fixed refuelling probe and extended gear are approximated from public three-view drawings. Materials are matte two-tone grey with low-visibility markings. Generator: `fighterGeometry.ts` via `pnpm --filter @fleet-maintenance/web model:aircraft`, **3,602,116 bytes**, SHA-256 `1e0a41ce216bc7c9877347c1a503429593cc0c4131f6fe733e8e0e168d941984`, 173 meshes and 145,816 triangles; root extras keep `engineeringValidated: false`.

The cutaway (`aircraftCutaway.ts`, `fighterInternals.ts`) now shows representative internal structure: fuselage frames and longerons, multi-spar wing box with ribs, bifurcated intake ducts to the engine face, fuselage and integral wing fuel tanks, radar antenna and transmitter, avionics racks, afterburner section, accessory gearbox and power take-off shaft, internal gun, and hydraulic, fuel and electrical runs. Recorded components are placed in plausible installation zones at real-world size (`componentArchitecture.ts`). `componentStructure.ts` models each type as such hardware is built (bladed fan/HPC spools with stator rows, combustor and turbine stages, axial-piston pump, wound generator, servo actuator, oleo strut, carbon brake stack, card-cage LRU, and so on).

None of this is manufacturer CAD, a configuration-controlled airframe or verified installation data. It is a public-information approximation for orientation and must not be used for installation, clearance, loads or physics. No third-party mesh, texture or drawing was copied.

## Authored illustrative fighter — 2026-10-09

After explicit user approval of a visual-only Tejas-inspired model, the fleet-health aircraft studio uses `apps/web/public/models/tejas-visual.glb`. Original geometry is authored in `apps/web/src/features/ops/fighterGeometry.ts` and reproducibly exported with Node 24 using `node apps/web/scripts/build-fighter-model.mjs` and the installed Three.js GLTFExporter. No third-party mesh, image, model weights or CAD was incorporated. The public [HAL Tejas brochure](https://hal-india.co.in/backend/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/LCA.pdf) informed the tailless compound-delta visual direction; it is not a dimension/configuration validation source, nor was its artwork copied. Decorative roundels imply no operational identity or government endorsement.

Visual details: shaped fuselage/radome and pitot, compound-delta wings/elevons, swept single fin/rudder, cockpit canopy/frame/coaming/headrest, paired intake mouths/lips/splitters, separate exhaust collars/petals, tricycle gear/tyres/hubs/oleos/braces/bay doors, empty pylons/rails, antenna/nav lenses, seams and instanced fasteners. The texture-free GLB is **1,644,344 bytes**, SHA-256 `2b8708c96fd91e127b6050c999da490227b032f4e3146dce46ae0c86a369b7d6`; generator traversal counts 123 mesh objects and 61,196 base triangles (instanced fasteners repeat their base geometry). Root extras explicitly retain `engineeringValidated: false` and arbitrary scene units. The local `fighter-poster.svg` is an authored simplified silhouette for renderer/model failure, not a CAD drawing or capture of the detailed GLB.

This model is a visual approximation with no verified dimensions, physical internals, configuration baseline, tolerances, aerodynamic accuracy or component installation mapping. Synthetic records remain independent of its one-engine visual shape. The original transport GLB/poster and licence are preserved for the untouched legacy inspection workflow; no source geometry or labels there are silently replaced.
